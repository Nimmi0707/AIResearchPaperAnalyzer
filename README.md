# 🔬 AI Research Paper Analyzer

> An interactive web application designed to help students and researchers analyze, understand, and explore academic research papers through an intuitive AI-powered interface.

---

## 🌟 Overview

AI Research Paper Analyzer is a modern web application that organizes important information from research papers into an easy-to-understand interactive dashboard.

Users can explore important academic information such as the abstract, keywords, methodology, algorithms, datasets, findings, and results through a structured interface.

The application also provides interactive features such as Ask My Paper and Research Paper Comparison.

## 📌 Main Modules

### Overview
Research paper summary and key information.

### Summary
Abstract and important research findings.

### Keywords
Important research keywords with visualizations.

### Methodology
Research methods, algorithms, and datasets.

### Results
Research results and interactive visualizations.

### Ask My Paper
Interactive question-and-answer interface for exploring the paper.

### Compare Papers
Compare two research papers and explore their differences and similarities.
